It’s reported that road collisions kill approximately 1.3 million people and injure tens of millions more every year. It is of the utmost importance to leverage the advancements in various technologies including artificial intelligence (AI) to address this hidden, global epidemic. The use of V2X (vehicle-to-everything) enables communication between vehicles, as well as between vehicles and roadside communication systems for road safety.
